## UI scope and outcome

The [independent critique](2026-09-22-ui-critique.md) covers all 40 templates and
shared UI code. Impeccable guided a bounded polish pass within DESIGN.md's
Werkblatt identity, not a redesign:

- Accessible, row-numbered quick entry, readable select widths and mobile scroll guidance.
- Direct invoice correction to the open neighbour editor; plain-language invoice guidance.
- Persistent actionable Undo messages, with ordinary status messages still transient.
- Wrapping financial explanations on the year-closing screen.
- Grouped drawer and document-output/delivery actions.
- Theme-correct skip/status contrast and visible legacy radio keyboard focus.
- Useful German numeric/email errors, preserving custom messages and help associations.

The final Impeccable detector reports **48 advisories/warnings**: 40 colour
advisories (39 fragment defaults plus an intentional signal-text colour), three
semantic accent rails, two radii, one root clipping boundary, one overlay shadow,
and the blueprint grid. These were checked against the actual cascade and pinned
design; they are not 48 unresolved product defects. The rendered contrast issue
was fixed. `.impeccable/design.json` is older than DESIGN.md; no unrelated design
metadata refresh was attempted.
